Frequently Asked Questions

Surface Pro is a 2-in-1 tablet with a detachable Type Cover keyboard and built-in kickstand, offering maximum portability and inking capabilities. Surface Laptop is a traditional clamshell laptop with an integrated keyboard, premium typing comfort, and longer battery endurance.

Surface Pro and Surface Go tablets are sold as standalone devices to allow users to customize their preferred keyboard color and pen. Surface Slim Pen 2 and Signature Type Covers are available as optional accessories or in bundle packages.

All new Surface devices purchased from the official store include a 1-year limited hardware warranty, 90 days of complimentary technical support, and a 60-day free return guarantee with zero restocking fees.
